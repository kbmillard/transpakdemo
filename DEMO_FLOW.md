# TransPak AI Command Center Demo Flow

This repo is an **operational demo** using **local seeded data** and **browser localStorage** for field updates. It is not connected to live TransPak systems.

## Quick walkthrough

1. Open **`/`** — the TransPak AI Command Center (operational dashboard).
2. Review the **KPI strip** and **Next best actions** (right column).
3. Scan **Public lead intelligence**, **Quote intake queue**, and **Active jobs** tables.
4. Open **`/qr`** and scan or tap a QR target (e.g. TPK-1001).
5. On **`/jobs/TPK-1001`**, use **ScanFlow actions**: status, note/photo filename, **Generate AI summary**, **Save update**.
6. Open **`/customer-updates/TPK-1001`**, copy the draft, mark approved locally.
7. Open **`/portal/CUST-001`** for the read-only customer portal example.
8. Return to **`/`** and confirm **Recent ScanFlow updates** lists your saved update.
9. Open **`/demo/quote-to-shop`** for the guided operational workflow.
10. Optional: **`/proposal-reference`** holds an archived proposal-style page (not linked from the app).

## Honest positioning

Demo uses **public lead examples** and **realistic job data** until connected to approved internal systems. **Human approval stays in the loop.**
